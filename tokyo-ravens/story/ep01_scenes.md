# Stage 7 — Scene Planning: Episode 1 "Young Ravens' Academy"

**Input:** `story/vol2_ep_outline.md` (Stage 6, Ep1) + `story/vol2_beat_sheet.md` (beats 1-1→1-6).
**Rule:** each scene lists location, time of day, characters present, scene goal (what changes by scene end), and entry/exit emotional beat. NO dialogue (Stage 8).

**CR-1 baseline:** Natsume male-presenting throughout Ep1 ("Natsume-kun").

## SCENE LIST

### S1-01 — Classroom, before the transfer
- **Location:** Onmyo Academy classroom (concert-hall style, raised rows)
- **Time:** Morning (before homeroom)
- **Characters:** Kyouko Kurahashi, Natsume Tsuchimikado (M)
- **Goal:** Establish Natsume's isolation and Kyouko's grudge; plant the Yakou-reincarnation rumor as the force shaping Natsume's academy life.
- **Entry emotion:** Kyouko — hopeful/nostalgic (approaching an old acquaintance). Natsume — composed, aloof.
- **Exit emotion:** Kyouko — shocked, stung by cold rejection. Natsume — guarded, distantly polite, already bracing for distance.

### S1-02 — Approaching the Academy
- **Location:** Academy building exterior (granite + scarlet window frames, new school building)
- **Time:** Morning (shortly after S1-01)
- **Characters:** Harutora Tsuchimikado, Touji Ato
- **Goal:** Introduce the two transfer students; establish Harutora's "outsider" anxiety and Touji's dry-worldly contrast; set the Tsuchimikado-name tension.
- **Entry emotion:** Harutora — disbelief/awed at the building. Touji — casual curiosity.
- **Exit emotion:** Harutora — nervous ("I'll be eaten"); Touji — amused, mildly warning.

### S1-03 — The komainu at the gate
- **Location:** Academy entrance interior (between the two automatic door sets)
- **Time:** Morning
- **Characters:** Harutora, Touji, komainu shikigami Alpha (right) & Omega (left, horned)
- **Goal:** Introduce the principal's high-level manmade shikigami guards; record voiceprint/aura; deliver the first hint that a shikigami is already bound to Harutora (Kon). Redirect boys to principal's office.
- **Entry emotion:** Harutora/Touji — joking about talking statues.
- **Exit emotion:** Harutora — startled/uneasy (the "shikigami already registered" line); Touji — intrigued, wary.

### S1-04 — The Principal's office
- **Location:** Principal's office (top floor; Taisho-cafe atmosphere, two-wall bookshelf, calico cat)
- **Time:** Mid-morning
- **Characters:** Harutora, Touji, Principal Kurahashi Miyo, her calico cat shikigami
- **Goal:** Reveal Miyo admitted them as a personal exception tied to the hushed-up Suzuka incident; warn of the Yakou-worshipping fanatics; compare the boys to Hishamaru/Kakugyouki; disclose she knew Yakou ("liked shogi, badly"); establish magic-community politics/danger.
- **Entry emotion:** Harutora — child-meeting-grandmother awkwardness. Touji — assessing.
- **Exit emotion:** Harutora — unsettled, newly aware of the weight on Natsume; Touji — resolved, "fish to water."

### S1-05 — Homeroom teacher Ohtomo
- **Location:** Principal's office → corridor
- **Time:** Mid-morning
- **Characters:** above + Ohtomo Jin (enters); later a lanky man with wooden fake leg, Kansai accent
- **Goal:** Introduce Ohtomo; he explains Hishamaru/Kakugyouki were Yakou's two retainers/shikigami; escorts boys toward class.
- **Entry emotion:** Harutora — confused by "Hishamaru/Kakugyouki" reference.
- **Exit emotion:** Harutora — bemused by Ohtomo's oddness; Touji — quietly respectful of his capability.

### S1-06 — Walk to class (corridor beats)
- **Location:** Academy corridor (museum-like, cursed-object displays, spotless)
- **Time:** Late morning
- **Characters:** Harutora, Touji, Ohtomo
- **Goal:** Ohtomo's "don't judge by appearance" + casual cut-class tip; plant that the principal's shikigami are everywhere; Touji quietly explains the Kurahashi weight to Harutora.
- **Entry emotion:** Harutora — still processing the principal.
- **Exit emotion:** Harutora — wary of the academy's unseen eyes; Touji — entertained.

### S1-07 — Classroom entrance / Kyouko's challenge
- **Location:** Classroom (full cast assembled)
- **Time:** Late morning (homeroom)
- **Characters:** Harutora, Touji, Natsume (M, black uniform, pink-ribboned ponytail), Kyouko, Ohtomo, rest of class (incl. Momoe Tenma)
- **Goal:** Assemble the main cast; Kyouko objects to the "special treatment" transfer; Natsume fiercely defends Harutora as her shikigami and asserts the Tsuchimikado name carries no unfair privilege; establish core class antagonism.
- **Entry emotion:** Harutora — intimidated by the staring class. Natsume — tense, ready to shield.
- **Exit emotion:** Harutora — relieved Natsume claimed him, but thrown by her ferocity; Kyouko — simmering, unconvinced; class — curious/divided. (Episode end / cold hook into Ep2.)
