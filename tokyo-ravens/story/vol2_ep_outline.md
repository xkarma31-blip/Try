# Stage 6 — Chapter/Episode Planning: Tokyo Ravens Vol.2 "RAVEN'S NEST"

**Input:** `story/vol2_beat_sheet.md` (Stage 5) + `data/timeline.json`.
**Plan model:** ONE LN chapter per episode (5 episodes). Rationale: each chapter carries
enough beats for a full ~22-min episode; clean 1:1 mapping keeps per-episode scene lists
(Stage 7) manageable and the QC gate simple.

**Pacing reference (NOT primary):** the Eight Bit anime condensed Vol.2 into ~3 episodes
(eps 4–6: RAVEN'S NEST -School / -Bonds / days in nest -Holiday) and bled into Vol.3.
Our 5-episode plan is the LN-faithful split; if an anime-style 3-episode cut is later
required, beats can be re-merged (noted per episode).

**CR-1 assumption for this plan:** Natsume remains male-presenting in public through the
volume; her female voice surfaces only in the private Ch.3 blow-up (beat 3-7). This matches
the primary text. CR-1 (early vs. deferred reveal) remains OPEN for creative-direction
sign-off — if changed, only Episode 3's 3-7 and Episode 5's dorm beats shift.

- **Acts (~22 min):**
  - Cold open (2): Kyouko's classroom reunion with Natsume — establishes isolation + rivalry. [1-1]
  - Act 1 (6): Harutora & Touji arrive; Touji's "you'll be eaten" warning; Alpha & Omega at the gate. [1-2, 1-3]
  - Act 2 (8): Principal's office — Miyo, the Suzuka-incident exception, Yakou fanatics warning, Hishamaru/Kakugyouki comparison. [1-4]
  - Act 3 (5): Ohtomo intro; classroom entrance; Kyouko's challenge; Natsume defends Harutora. [1-5, 1-6]
- **Locations:** Academy exterior/entrance, principal's office, classroom.
- **Characters introduced:** Harutora, Touji, Natsume(M), Kyouko, Miyo, Ohtomo, Alpha/Omega, Tenma (cameo).
- **Hook/tag:** Harutora's shikigami mark + the "shikigami already registered" mystery (Kon).
- **Anime-condense note:** merges with opening of Ep.2 if doing a 3-ep cut.
