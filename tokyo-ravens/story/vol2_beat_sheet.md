## CANON CONFIRMATIONS (adaptation-critical facts from text)
- Natsume presents MALE per family tradition (black uniform, black hair + pink ribbon, "Natsume-kun"); aloof/isolated due to Yakou-reincarnation rumor. Peers read "him" as a prodigy boy.
- Harutora bears a pentagram shikigami-mark under his LEFT EYE; spiritual power "far greater than average."
- Komainu guards **Alpha** (right) & **Omega** (left, horned) at academy entrance; record voiceprint/aura; noted a shikigami already bound to Harutora (Kon).
- Principal **Kurahashi Miyo**: gray-haired, head of Kurahashi family (top Onmyoudou house), former acquaintance of Yakou ("liked shogi, badly"); calico cat shikigami shares her voice. (Kurahashi family was originally a Tsuchimikado branch family.)
- Homeroom teacher **Ohtomo Jin**: wooden pirate-style fake leg, Kansai accent, ex-Mystical Investigator, National First-Class Onmyouji (Twelve Divine Generals, name withheld); crafts enchanted tools (breaking wooden sword, impact-absorbing shakujou); uses Unmoving Golden Chains / Marici Stealth.
- **Kon**: fox-eared/tailed child shikigami, descendant of fox Kuzunoha; wields wakizashi "Kachiwari"; addresses Harutora as "Harutora-sama"; the "something unfamiliar" he brought.
- **Touji**: spiritual-disaster side-effects, bandanna, former delinquent, self-taught.
- **Suzuka Dairenji is OFF-PAGE in Vol.2** — referenced only (summer incident that pulled Harutora/Touji in). CONFIRMS Stage-4 constraint: do NOT seat her as a Vol.2 classmate.
- **Yakou-worshipping fanatics / Twin-Horned Syndicate**: deify Yakou, call Natsume "North Star King"; one (suit-wearing Mystical Investigator, self-id "Hishamaru") is the Vol.2 antagonist, controlling a fake "Kakugyouki" (one-armed oni). Old man "Doman" implied deeper puppet-master.
- **Hokuto** (golden dragon, Tsuchimikado guardian servant shikigami) summoned by Natsume only after Harutora tears her sealing charms; distinct from the summer friend "Hokuto."
- **Kyouko** (Hakuou/Kokfuu, G2 Yaksha defensive shikigami) and **Tenma** ARE present in Vol.2 as classmates — confirmed, not later additions.
- Magic system: First-class (Agency-recognized) vs Second-class (rumors/forbidden/divination); shikigami classes: servant / manmade (general/standard/high-level); kodoku = forbidden curse-type bug-vessel.
