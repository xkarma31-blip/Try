## SCENE LIST

### S3-01 — Arena & plan
- **Location:** Underground magic training field (arena, spectator seats)
- **Time:** Daytime (declared training session)
- **Characters:** Class — Touji & Tenma in stands; Natsume (M) watching, cold; Ohtomo (referee); Harutora, Kon, Kyouko, Hakuou, Kokfuu
- **Goal:** Set the battle venue (National First-Class barrier, can contain a 3rd-phase spiritual disaster); Touji/Tenma learn defensive shikigami are status symbols requiring strong spiritual power — reframing why the class is shocked Harutora has one.
- **Entry:** Class — expectant. Harutora — nervous but determined.
- **Exit:** Class — realizing Harutora's shikigami is significant; tension set.

### S3-02 — Harutora's armored entrance
- **Location:** Arena
- **Time:** Daytime
- **Characters:** Harutora (kendo protector + miasma gear + enchanted wooden sword from Ohtomo), Kon, Kyouko
- **Goal:** Harutora intends to fight personally alongside Kon, treating himself as "also a shikigami"; establishes his unorthodox, self-deprecating combat approach and Ohtomo's engineered "reputation-restoring" plan.
- **Entry:** Harutora — resolved.
- **Exit:** Kyouko — scornful/challenged; class — divided between laughter and concern.

### S3-03 — Showdown (Harutora loses)
- **Location:** Arena
- **Time:** Daytime
- **Characters:** Harutora, Kon vs. Kyouko's Hakuou & Kokfuu; Ohtomo refs; class watches
- **Goal:** Harutora is spectacularly defeated (wooden sword shatters on Yaksha armor); demonstrate the power gap and the G2 Yaksha; Touji notes Harutora was setting up a finishing blow the broken sword foiled; class laughs; Natsume furious (worried).
- **Entry:** Combat — energetic.
- **Exit:** Harutora — defeated, sheepish; Natsume — furious/worried; class — amused.

### S3-04 — Locker-room confrontation
- **Location:** Locker room
- **Time:** Post-match
- **Characters:** Harutora, Kon, Natsume (M), Touji
- **Goal:** Natsume scolds Harutora's recklessness; Harutora snaps "Is that an order, master?"; Natsume runs off in tears/anger. First open fracture in their relationship; Touji notes "she said that because she was worried."
- **Entry:** Natsume — scolding. Harutora — defensive.
- **Exit:** Natsume — fleeing, hurt; Harutora — rattled, regretful.

### S3-05 — Morning acceptance
- **Location:** Classroom (next morning)
- **Time:** Next morning
- **Characters:** Harutora, Kon, Tenma, classmates (incl. a hostile male student), Natsume (M, watching from seat), Touji
- **Goal:** After the fight, classmates warm to Harutora; he shows off Kon, apologizes to the hostile boy, learns names; Natsume watches, excluded and hurt; Touji warns her not to "force herself" and references Hokuto (a friend who died fighting because of forcing herself).
- **Entry:** Harutora — lighter, accepted. Natsume — isolated, watching.
- **Exit:** Natsume — quietly aching; Harutora — unaware of her hurt.

### S3-06 — Kyouko's apology / childhood truth
- **Location:** Emergency stairwell (noon break)
- **Time:** Midday
- **Characters:** Harutora, Kon (hidden), Kyouko
- **Goal:** Kyouko apologizes; reveals she and Natsume met as small children and made a promise Natsume forgot; reveals Kurahashi was originally a Tsuchimikado branch family; Harutora explains he CHOSE to become Natsume's shikigami (not family coercion), shifting Kyouko's view of him.
- **Entry:** Kyouko — guilty, defensive. Harutora — wary.
- **Exit:** Kyouko — softening; Harutora — sympathetic; tentative rapport.

### S3-07 — Natsume finds them / blow-up
- **Location:** Emergency stairwell → corridor
- **Time:** Midday (immediately after S3-06)
- **Characters:** Harutora, Kyouko, Natsume (M → flees in TRUE female voice), suit Mystical Investigator
- **Goal:** Natsume discovers them, misinterprets Harutora "cozying up" to Kyouko, berates his "fawning" approach; Harutora explodes, accusing her of being too scared to connect with others; Natsume flees in her true (female) voice; the Mystical Investigator stuns her with a "Stupefy, seal, bind" charm and carries her off.
- **Entry:** Natsume — jealous/accusing. Harutora — defensive, then angry.
- **Exit:** Harutora — horrified (her voice/abduction); CR-1 female-voice beat lands here, private/transient; hook into Ep4. (CR-1 still OPEN for sign-off.)
