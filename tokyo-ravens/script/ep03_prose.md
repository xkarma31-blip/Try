# Stage 9 — Light Novel Narration: Episode 3 "Shikigami Showdown"

**Style (§5.5):** third-person limited (Harutora PoV), restrained interiority, dry humor.

---

The training field lay underground, ringed by spectator seats and wrapped in a First-Class
barrier built to hold a third-phase spiritual disaster. Harutora, padded in kendo armor and
miasma gear with Ohtomo's enchanted wooden sword, stepped into the ring beside Kon and told
Kyouko he meant to fight too—not just as Natsume's shikigami, but as himself.

The wooden sword shattered against the Yaksha's armor. Kyouko's G2 familiars didn't even
flinch. From the stands, Touji noted that Harutora had been lining up a finishing blow the
instant the sword broke; the class, less charitable, laughed. Natsume, watching, looked like
she wanted to murder someone—probably Harutora.

In the locker room she nearly did. "Reckless!" she hissed. "Is that an order, master?" he
snapped back, and she fled, stung. Touji, ever helpful, explained she'd only said it because
she was worried.

The next morning the class warmed to him. He showed off Kon, apologized to the boy who'd
glared at him, learned names. Natsume watched from her seat, excluded and aching, and Touji
told her not to force herself—remembering Hokuto, who'd died pushing herself too far.

At noon, on the emergency stairwell, Kyouko apologized. She and Natsume had met as children;
Natsume had promised her something and forgotten. The Kurahashi family, she revealed, had
once been a Tsuchimikado branch. And Harutora—Harutora had chosen to be Natsume's shikigami.
Not duty. Choice.

Then Natsume found them. Misread the closeness. Accused him of fawning. Harutora, furious,
threw it back: you're the one too scared to let anyone close. And for one breath, Natsume's
composure cracked—her voice rising into a register it shouldn't have had—before the
Mystical Investigator stepped from the shadows, sealed her with "Stupefy, seal, bind," and
carried her into the dark.

Harutora stared after the empty corridor, the wrongness of that voice still ringing.
