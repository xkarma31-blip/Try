## S3-01 — Arena & plan
**Touji:** So defensive shikigami are status symbols. That's why the class gawked at yours.
**Tenma:** Only the strong can bind one. Natsume-kun and Kyouko-san are the only first-years who have.
**Harutora:** Then Kon's... pretty amazing, huh.
**Ohtomo:** *(referee's whistle)* Arena's a First-Class barrier—contains a third-phase disaster. Fight clean, ya?

## S3-02 — Harutora's armored entrance
**Harutora:** I'm not just Natsume's shikigami. I'm fighting too. Kon, let's do this!
**Kyouko:** Foolish. A wooden sword against a Yaksha?
**Kon:** Harutora-sama, I will protect you, na no!

## S3-03 — Showdown (Harutora loses)
*Wooden sword shatters on Yaksha armor.*
**Kyouko:** Done already? I expected more from a Tsuchimikado.
**Harutora:** *(panting)* Tch... I had a finishing blow lined up, y'know...
**Touji:** *(from stands)* The sword broke before you could land it. Dumb luck, as always.
**Natsume:** *(furious, under breath)* That idiot...

## S3-04 — Locker-room confrontation
**Natsume:** Reckless! You could have been hurt—what were you thinking?!
**Harutora:** Is that an order, master?
**Natsume:** ...! *(flees, stung)*
**Touji:** She said that 'cause she was worried, y'know.

## S3-05 — Morning acceptance
**Harutora:** Hey—sorry 'bout yesterday. I'm Harutora. This is Kon, na no—I mean, Kon.
**Classmate (hostile boy):** ...Tch. Whatever. You're alright, I guess.
**Natsume:** *(watching from seat, quiet)* ...
**Touji:** *(to Natsume)* Don't force yourself. Remember Hokuto—she died pushing herself too.

## S3-06 — Kyouko's apology / childhood truth
**Kyouko:** I'm sorry. For the challenge. We... met as kids. At the Tsuchimikado estate. You promised me something. You forgot.
**Harutora:** Natsume forgot? That's... like him.
**Kyouko:** The Kurahashi family was once a Tsuchimikado branch family. And you—you chose to be his shikigami, didn't you? Not because of the family.
**Harutora:** Yeah. I chose. He's my friend.

## S3-07 — Natsume finds them / blow-up
**Natsume:** *(jealous)* So this is why you cozy up to classmates. Fawning over Kyouko now?
**Harutora:** Hah?! I'm not fawning—I was just talking!
**Natsume:** You're too scared to connect with anyone! You hide behind me!
**Harutora:** And you're too scared to let anyone close!
*Natsume's composure breaks; her voice slips to its true, higher register as she flees.*
**Natsume:** *(female voice, breaking)* You—! 
*Suit Mystical Investigator appears, stuns her with "Stupefy, seal, bind," carries her off.*
**Harutora:** Natsume—?! That voice—!
