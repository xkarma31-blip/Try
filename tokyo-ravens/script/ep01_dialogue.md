## S1-01 — Classroom, before the transfer
*Kyouko approaches Natsume at his seat.*

**Kyouko:** I-It's been a while, Natsume-kun. Do you still remember me? I'm Kurahashi Kyouko, from the Kurahashi family.

**Natsume:** Kurahashi... Right. Is there something you need from me?

**Kyouko:** We met as children, at the Tsuchimikado estate—surely you recall?

**Natsume:** *(cool)* I don't recall any such thing. If there's nothing else, I'd prefer to be alone.

**Kyouko:** *(stung)* ...I see. Of course.

## S1-02 — Approaching the Academy
*Harutora and Touji stand before the new school building.*

**Harutora:** Huh, here? This building? It's really the Academy? ...I still feel like an outsider.

**Touji:** You better prepare your heart.

**Harutora:** I was prepared to become an Onmyouji long ago. Probably.

**Touji:** Didn't you just correct yourself?

**Harutora:** Since I've got the spirit-seeing ability now and I'm Natsume's shikigami, of course I prepared myself! ...Probably.

**Touji:** *(dry)* Don't be naive. You're a Tsuchimikado, but a branch one. Everyone here reacts the moment they hear the name. Some out of curiosity, some out of jealousy, some hoping to make a name by beating a Tsuchimikado. You might get "eaten."

**Harutora:** B-But Natsume's here too—

**Touji:** Mendokusai. The main-family heir's a prodigy everyone admires. You showing up late as a branch nobody? You'll draw every eye in the place.

**Harutora:** *(pale)* ...I'm just an outsider!

**Touji:** Doesn't matter. All they care about is the name Tsuchimikado.

## S1-03 — The komainu at the gate
*Between the two automatic doors. The stone komainu speak.*

**Alpha:** Welcome to the Onmyo Academy. State your names.

**Harutora:** I'm Tsuchimikado Harutora.

**Touji:** Ato Touji.

**Omega:** The voiceprint and aura of Tsuchimikado Harutora and Ato Touji have been confirmed and recorded. We have also recorded your shikigami.

**Harutora:** My shikigami? I don't have any shikigami!

**Touji:** ...Maybe it's referring to you being Natsume's shikigami?

**Harutora:** That's way too strange a way to put it. Alpha, say it clearly—

**Omega:** Our master has notified us: proceed directly to the principal's office.

## S1-04 — The Principal's office
*Miyo with her calico cat. Taisho-cafe room.*

**Miyo:** Welcome. I've been waiting a long time for you two. I am the principal here, Kurahashi Miyo.

**Harutora:** H-Hello.

**Miyo:** So you are Natsume-san's Hishamaru and Kakugyouki.

**Harutora:** What?!

**Touji:** *(watching her)* ...Interesting choice of words.

**Miyo:** I admitted you both as a personal exception, tied to the Dairenji Suzuka incident this summer. I won't deny I also wished to seal your silence on the matter.

**Harutora:** A-As I thought...

**Miyo:** You should know the rumors regarding Natsume-san — that he is Tsuchimikado Yakou's reincarnation. Because of them, he is hounded. By a group that worships Yakou as a god. They have even tried to contact him.

**Harutora:** Th-They came for Natsume? How—?

**Miyo:** *(nostalgic)* He really liked playing shogi. Terribly bad at it, mind you. ...Yes, I knew Yakou. Half a century ago, I was only a child.

**Harutora:** You MET Yakou?!

**Touji:** You saw Tsuchimikado Yakou when he was alive?

## S1-05 — Homeroom teacher Ohtomo
*Ohtomo limps in on a wooden fake leg, Kansai lilt.*

**Ohtomo:** Oh? This? It's cool, isn't it? I'm a teacher here, but I'm also an Onmyouji, so I've gotta boast about my prestige.

**Miyo:** This is your homeroom teacher, Ohtomo Jin-sensei. Don't judge him by his appearance—he's extremely capable.

**Ohtomo:** How can you say that, principal. Ah, whatever—hope we get along.

**Harutora:** S-Sensei, can I ask something? The principal said "Hishamaru" and "Kakugyouki"—what do those refer to?

**Ohtomo:** Harutora-san, they were the shikigami of Tsuchimikado Yakou. His two retainers.

**Harutora:** Huh?!

## S1-06 — Walk to class (corridor)
*Ohtomo leads them; the cat trails.*

**Ohtomo:** Wasn't she scary? That old lady's a big behind-the-scenes figure in this industry.

**Harutora:** The principal? You're talking about the principal?

**Ohtomo:** Right. Her shikigami are everywhere, so you'll be caught if you cut class. But if you ever need to—come ask me. I'll tip you off on how to slip past her eyes and ears.

**Touji:** *(aside to Harutora)* So it was that Kurahashi. I'll explain later.

## S1-07 — Classroom entrance / Kyouko's challenge
*Full class stares. Kyouko confronts Harutora.*

**Kyouko:** A branch-family Tsuchimikado, transferring in now? After the main-family heir? That's special treatment if I've ever seen it.

**Natsume:** *(stepping forward, commanding)* He is my shikigami. The Tsuchimikado name grants no privilege he has not earned. Bakatora—stand behind me.

**Harutora:** Y-Yes, Natsume!

**Kyouko:** *(narrowing eyes)* ...We'll see whether a late transfer lasts a week.

**Harutora:** *(under his breath)* I'm really gonna get eaten, aren't I...

**Touji:** *(deadpan)* Told you.
