## S4-01 — Waiting / the shakujou
**Tenma:** Ohtomo-sensei sent this. His shakujou—the one with the ringing rings.
**Harutora:** Natsume still hasn't shown. Where'd that suit guy take her?
**Touji:** Mendokusai. Sit tight. He'll surface.

## S4-02 — Kodoku attack / sealed classroom
*Window shatters; a bug-vessel curse shikigami floods in; barrier seals the room.*
**Kon:** Harutora-sama, behind me, na no!
**Kyouko:** That's a kodoku—forbidden curse-type! This is Yakou-fanatic work!
**Harutora:** They're after me?! Why—?
**Touji:** Questions later. Move!

## S4-03 — Truth about Natsume's "special classes"
**Kyouko:** That suit man's a Mystical Investigator. The Agency's been watching Natsume-kun.
**Tenma:** A fanatic ambushed Natsume-kun two days before you even enrolled. Tried to "awaken" him. The Agency's investigating.
**Harutora:** *(fist clenching)* He bore that alone... I'm gonna reach him.
**Touji:** Counter-curse. I'll trigger the sprinklers to thin the aura—you two cast.

## S4-04 — Natsume captive / "Hishamaru" revealed
*Natsume bound in the arena; Investigator looms.*
**Investigator:** North Star King. The King has returned to you. I am Hishamaru, your loyal retainer.
**Natsume:** I am Tsuchimikado Natsume. I am no one's king.
**Investigator:** Deny it if you will. My comrades within the Agency await. And your shikigami... Harutora breathes his last even now.
**Natsume:** *(struggling)* Harutora...! I can't reach his aura—the barrier, the seals—

## S4-05 — Counter-curse & rescue
**Touji:** Now—sprinklers!
**Kyouko:** *(casting)* Return, curse, to your caster!
**Tenma:** *(breaking the classroom barrier)* Got it—!
*Rebounding fog proves the caster.*
**Investigator:** Impossible... the vessel turned on me?
**Harutora:** *(arriving with the group)* We're taking Natsume back.

## S4-06 — One-armed oni appears
**Investigator:** *(enraged)* You name HIM your only true shikigami?! Then witness—Kakugyouki!
*Giant one-armed oni materializes.*
**Investigator:** I am Hishamaru. This is Kakugyouki—Yakou's two retainers, reborn!
*Cliffhanger.*
