## S2-01 — Trio talk / Natsume pulled away
**Harutora:** Glad that's over... Touji, you and Natsume hit it off weirdly fast.
**Touji:** My cat was named Touji. Not my fault the guy remembers a cat better'n a person.
**Natsume:** *(brisk)* Harutora. I'm called to a special class. Don't cause trouble while I'm gone.
**Harutora:** H-hey, where are you—?
*Suit Mystical Investigator appears; collects Natsume.*

## S2-02 — Recon with Tenma
**Tenma:** ...So the Kurahashi family is the top Onmyoudou house now. The principal is its head. Kyouko-san is her granddaughter.
**Harutora:** The principal's granddaughter? No wonder she's confident.
**Tenma:** Natsume-kun and Kyouko-san are the two best in class. The only first-years with defensive shikigami. Kyouko-san... targets Natsume-kun specifically.
**Touji:** *(dry)* So it's a rivalry. Mendokusai.

## S2-03 — Afternoon classes expose Harutora's ignorance
**Harutora:** Wait, "first-class magic" is an Agency license? I thought everyone just... did magic.
**Natsume:** *(through clenched calm)* You truly know nothing. After class. I'm tutoring you.
**Harutora:** Aww, Natsume—
**Natsume:** Don't "Aww" me. You're a disgrace to the name.
*Suit man reappears; whisks Natsume away again.*

## S2-04 — Kon's manifestation
*Harutora opens his father's talisman; light resolves into a small fox-eared girl.*
**Kon:** Harutora-sama! At last, we meet face to face, na no!
**Harutora:** Y-You're... my shikigami? From my old man's charm?
**Kon:** Indeed! I am Kon, a defensive shikigami descended of the fox Kuzunoha. My blade is Kachiwari, na no!
**Harutora:** A talking fox kid with a sword. Hokuto, you'd've laughed at this.
**Kon:** Harutora-sama belongs to me now. I shall guard you with my life, na no!

## S2-05 — Natsume's misinterpretation
*Natsume bursts in as Kon unties her hakama.*
**Natsume:** H-Harutora?! What are you—! You pervert!
**Harutora:** W-What?! Natsume, it's not what it—!
**Kon:** Harutora-sama, this rude person is—?
**Natsume:** *(charms flying)* Don't you "Harutora-sama" him! Bakatora, I'll kill you!
*Harutora "dies" (per Touji). Natsume storms off, refusing to speak to him next day.*

## S2-06 — Antagonist safehouse (POV only)
**Fanatic A:** The vessel is primed. The cursed earth sits quiet.
**Fanatic B:** The King has confirmed his identity. Soon, the North Star King awakens.
**Fanatic A:** *(incanting over a sealed pot)* Kodoku... nourish. The time approaches.

## S2-07 — Ohtomo review / Kon's debut
**Ohtomo:** Right, laggards—review time! Harutora-kun, you're hopeless, so listen close, ya?
**Kyouko:** Special treatment again? He transferred in on an exception!
**Harutora:** I didn't ask for—!
**Kyouko:** If you can't keep up, just drop out.
*Kon bursts from hiding.*
**Kon:** No one insults Harutora-sama, na no!
**Kyouko:** A shikigami?! Then face mine—Hakuou! Kokfuu!
**Ohtomo:** *(grinning)* A shikigami showdown, then. Let's give 'em a match.
