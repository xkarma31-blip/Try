# Stage 9 — Light Novel Narration: Episode 2 "Ears and Tail"

**Style (§5.5):** third-person limited (Harutora PoV), restrained interiority, dry humor.

---

Noon light fell across the classroom, and for a moment Harutora let himself think the
transfer might not be a disaster. Then the suit-wearing man appeared at the door, and Natsume
was pulled away again for one of his "special classes," leaving Harutora and Touji to trade
shrugs with Tenma Momoe, who quietly explained that the Kurahashi family ran the entire
Onmyoudou world now, that the principal was its head, and that Natsume and Kyouko were the
two best students—and the only first-years with defensive shikigami.

Afternoon classes dismantled any confidence Harutora had left. He didn't know what
first-class magic was, or second-class, or why a license mattered, and Natsume's eye twitch
as she scheduled yet another tutoring session told him he'd embarrassed her again. The suit
man took Natsume before Harutora could apologize.

That evening, in his six-tatami dorm room, Harutora broke the seal on the talisman his
father had pressed into his hand at parting. Light unfolded into a small girl with fox ears
and a fox tail, who looked up at him with reverent, shining eyes and announced herself as
Kon, his shikigami, descended of the fox Kuzunoha, blade named Kachiwari.

"Harutora-sama," she said, "na no!"

For the first time since Hokuto, something warm settled in his chest. Then the door slid
open and Natsume walked in—just as Kon, untangling her hakama, revealed a tail.

"You pervert!" Natsume shrieked, charms already flying. "Bakatora, I'll kill you!"

She left before he could explain, and didn't speak to him the next day. Touji later
confirmed, with patent enjoyment, that Harutora had in fact "died."

After dark, in an apartment no protagonist would ever visit, a sealed pot breathed cursed
earth, and a man intoned that the King had confirmed his identity. The plan advanced.

The following day, Ohtomo herded the laggards into review, Kyouko accused Harutora of
special treatment, and told him to drop out. Kon burst from hiding. Kyouko answered with
Hakuou and Kokfuu. Ohtomo, grinning, declared a shikigami showdown—and the academy's
quiet finally broke.
