## S5-01 — Kakugyouki vs Harutora
*Oni swipes; shakujou reflects the impact.*
**Natsume:** *(bound)* Halt! Don't throw your life away!
**Harutora:** Natsume—you don't have to carry everything alone! Rely on me. On all of us!
**Kon:** Harutora-sama, na no!
**Natsume:** *(softening)* ...Idiot.

## S5-02 — Coordinated assault
**Harutora:** Kyouko—flank left with Hakuou and Kokfuu! Through the blind spot!
**Kyouko:** If you say so, shikigami! Hakuou! Kokfuu—strike!
**Touji:** The shakujou's eating its aura. Wear it down!
**Harutora:** Now—!

## S5-03 — Mask broken / oni berserks / Investigator flees
*Harutora pierces the bronze mask; oni rampages.*
**Harutora:** Got the seal—!
**Investigator:** No—Kakugyouki, obey—! *[loses control, flees]*

## S5-04 — Rescue & Hokuto's summoning
*Harutora tears Natsume's sealing charms.*
**Harutora:** Natsume—come on, summon him!
**Natsume:** Hokuto!
*Golden dragon manifests, bites through the oni's neck; it dissipates.*

## S5-05 — The fake revealed
**Touji:** *(kicking debris)* This... a mass-market charm. The "Kakugyouki" was manmade. His Hishamaru act was a delusion.
**Natsume:** *(collapsing onto Harutora)* ...Thank you, Harutora.
**Harutora:** Heh. Told you. Not alone.

## S5-06 — Doman & the one-armed stranger (epilogue A)
**Doman:** *(young voice from old body)* A pity. The fanatic used the name, but the bait was sweet.
**Stranger (one-armed):** The tiger fought well. And the dragon at his side... the tiger and dragon, side by side.
**Ohtomo:** *(Unmoving Golden Chains)* That's far enough. Principal's been watching the whole time, ya?
*Principal-as-cat purrs from a ledge.*

## S5-07 — Dorm reunion / Kyouko's confession (epilogue B)
**Natsume:** I'm moving into the male dorm. Twenty-four-hour guard. As a guy, obviously.
**Kyouko:** Natsume-kun—! I love you! We made a promise, didn't we? Harutora, help me!
**Harutora:** W-Whoa, me?! I'm not getting involved!
**Natsume:** *(overhearing)* ...Playboy.

## S5-08 — Two memory codas
*Young Kyouko, courtyard:* **Young Kyouko:** My ribbon—! *Young Harutora:* I'll help you find it.
*Oni's memory, moonlit:* **Hishamaru (departing):** Wait for me, Kakugyouki. *Oni:* Always, my lord.
