# Stage 9 — Light Novel Narration: Episode 5 "One-Armed Oni"

**Style (§5.5):** third-person, climax register; restraint gives way to release. Memory
codas in a softer, wistful key.

---

The oni struck, and the shakujou rang as it turned the blow back. Bound and powerless,
Natsume screamed at Harutora to stop throwing his life away. He didn't. "You don't have to
carry everything alone," he said. "Rely on me. On all of us." Kon answered at his side;
Natsume, somewhere under the fear, softened.

They moved as a unit—Kyouko flanking through the arena's blind spot with Hakuou and
Kokfuu, the shakujou drinking the oni's aura. Harutora drove the enchanted ring into the
bronze mask, the oni's seal, and the giant went mad, tearing the arena apart. The
Investigator lost his grip and ran.

Harutora tore Natsume's sealing charms. "Summon him," he said. "Hokuto!" And the golden
dragon—the Tsuchimikado guardian—answered, biting through the oni's neck until it dissolved.

In the rubble, Touji kicked a mass-market charm. The "Kakugyouki" had been manmade; the
Investigator's Hishamaru was a delusion, nothing more. Natsume collapsed against Harutora
and whispered thanks.

That evening, in the parking lot, an old man with a young voice behind blood-red sunglasses
named Doman watched a one-armed stranger call Harutora "the tiger," and speak of the dragon
at his side—the tiger and dragon, side by side. Ohtomo's chains took the Investigator; the
principal's cat watched from a ledge. The bait had been the students, and the trap had
sprung on the Twin-Horned Syndicate instead.

Next day, Natsume moved into the male dorm "as a guy," and Kyouko declared her love and her
childhood promise, roping a flustered Harutora into helping—only for Natsume to overhear and
call him a playboy. And in memory: a little Kyouko who lost a ribbon, and a boy who helped
her find it; an oni beneath a moon, told by a departing partner to wait. Loyalty, and the
long patience of those who wait for their masters.
