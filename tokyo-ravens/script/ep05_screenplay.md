**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - TRAINING ARENA - AFTERNOON**

The oni swipes; the shakujou RINGS as it turns the blow back. Natsume bound.

**NATSUME**
Halt! Don't throw your life away!

**HARUTORA**
Natsume—you don't have to carry everything alone! Rely on me. On all of us!

**KON**
Harutora-sama, na no!

**NATSUME**
(softening)
...Idiot.

**HARUTORA**
Kyouko—flank left with Hakuou and Kokfuu! Through the blind spot!

**KYOUKO**
If you say so, shikigami! Hakuou! Kokfuu—strike!

**TOUJI**
The shakujou's eating its aura. Wear it down!

[Harutora drives the ring into the bronze mask—the oni's seal. The giant goes MAD, tearing the arena.]

**HARUTORA**
Got the seal—!

**INVESTIGATOR**
No—Kakugyouki, obey—! [flees]

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - TRAINING ARENA - CONT.**

Harutora tears Natsume's sealing charms.

**HARUTORA**
Natsume—come on, summon him!

**NATSUME**
Hokuto!

[A GOLDEN DRAGON manifests, bites through the oni's neck; it dissipates.]

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - ARENA AFTERMATH**

Touji kicks debris.

**TOUJI**
A mass-market charm. The "Kakugyouki" was manmade. His Hishamaru act was a delusion.

**NATSUME**
(collapsing onto Harutora)
...Thank you, Harutora.

**HARUTORA**
Heh. Told you. Not alone.

**EXT. ACADEMY PARKING LOT / ALLEY - EVENING**

DOMAN—old body, young voice, blood-red sunglasses—watches a ONE-ARMED STRANGER.

**STRANGER**
The tiger fought well. And the dragon at his side... the tiger and dragon, side by side.

**OHTOMO**
(unmoving golden chains)
That's far enough. Principal's been watching the whole time, ya?

[The principal's CAT purrs from a ledge.]

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - MALE DORM - NEXT DAY**

**NATSUME**
I'm moving into the male dorm. Twenty-four-hour guard. As a guy, obviously.

**KYOUKO**
Natsume-kun—! I love you! We made a promise, didn't we? Harutora, help me!

**HARUTORA**
W-Whoa, me?! I'm not getting involved!

**NATSUME**
(overhearing)
...Playboy.

**MONTAGE - MEMORY CODAS**

Young Kyouko, lost ribbon; young Harutora helps search. / Moonlit parting: Hishamaru departs, oni promised to wait.

**FADE OUT.**
