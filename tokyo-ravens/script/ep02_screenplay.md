**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CLASSROOM - NOON**

Harutora sags with relief. Natsume stands brisk.

**HARUTORA**
Glad that's over... Touji, you and Natsume hit it off weirdly fast.

**TOUJI**
My cat was named Touji. Not my fault the guy remembers a cat better'n a person.

**NATSUME**
Harutora. I'm called to a special class. Don't cause trouble while I'm gone.

[A SUITED MYSTICAL INVESTIGATOR appears; ushers Natsume out.]

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CLASSROOM - NOON (LATER)**

Harutora, Touji, TENMA at a desk.

**TENMA**
The Kurahashi family is the top Onmyoudou house now. The principal is its head. Kyouko-san is her granddaughter.

**HARUTORA**
The principal's granddaughter? No wonder she's confident.

**TENMA**
Natsume-kun and Kyouko-san are the two best. The only first-years with defensive shikigami. Kyouko-san targets Natsume-kun specifically.

**TOUJI**
(dry)
So it's a rivalry. Mendokusai.

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CLASSROOM - AFTERNOON**

**HARUTORA**
Wait, "first-class magic" is an Agency license? I thought everyone just... did magic.

**NATSUME**
(clenched calm)
You truly know nothing. After class. I'm tutoring you.

**HARUTORA**
Aww, Natsume—

**NATSUME**
Don't "Aww" me. You're a disgrace to the name.

[The suited man reappears; whisks Natsume away.]

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - MALE DORM ROOM - EVENING**

Harutora breaks a talisman. Light resolves into KON — fox ears, fox tail, a small blade.

**KON**
Harutora-sama! At last, we meet face to face, na no!

**HARUTORA**
Y-You're... my shikigami? From my old man's charm?

**KON**
Indeed! I am Kon, a defensive shikigami descended of the fox Kuzunoha. My blade is Kachiwari, na no!

**HARUTORA**
(soft)
A talking fox kid with a sword. Hokuto, you'd've laughed at this.

**KON**
Harutora-sama belongs to me now. I shall guard you with my life, na no!

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - MALE DORM ROOM - EVENING (CONT.)**

Natsume bursts in as Kon unties her hakama.

**NATSUME**
H-Harutora?! What are you—! You pervert!

**HARUTORA**
W-What?! Natsume, it's not what it—

**KON**
Harutora-sama, this rude person is—?

**NATSUME**
(charms flying)
Don't you "Harutora-sama" him! Bakatora, I'll kill you!

[Harutora "dies" (per Touji). Natsume storms off.]

**INT. FANATIC SAFEHOUSE - NIGHT**

A sealed pot breathes cursed earth.

**FANATIC A**
The vessel is primed.

**FANATIC B**
The King has confirmed his identity. Soon, the North Star King awakens.

**FANATIC A**
(incanting)
Kodoku... nourish. The time approaches.

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CLASSROOM / TRAINING FIELD - NEXT DAY**

Ohtomo herds laggards. Kyouko accuses.

**KYOUKO**
Special treatment again? He transferred in on an exception!

**HARUTORA**
I didn't ask for—!

**KYOUKO**
If you can't keep up, just drop out.

[KON bursts from hiding.]

**KON**
No one insults Harutora-sama, na no!

**KYOUKO**
A shikigami?! Then face mine—Hakuou! Kokfuu!

**OHTOMO**
(grinning)
A shikigami showdown, then. Let's give 'em a match.

**FADE OUT.**
