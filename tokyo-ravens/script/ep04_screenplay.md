# Stage 10 — Screenplay Adaptation: Episode 4 "Kodoku"

**Input:** `script/ep04_prose.md` + `script/ep04_dialogue.md`. Ch.4 = "Kodoku" (CR-6). Format per §5.6.

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CLASSROOM - AFTERNOON**

Natsume absent. Tenma hands Harutora a shakujou with ringing rings.

**TENMA**
Ohtomo-sensei sent this. His shakujou—the one with the ringing rings.

**HARUTORA**
Natsume still hasn't shown. Where'd that suit guy take her?

**TOUJI**
Mendokusai. Sit tight. He'll surface.

[SFX: window SHATTERS. A kodoku—bug-vessel curse shikigami—floods in. A barrier seals the room.]

**KYOUKO**
That's a kodoku—forbidden curse-type! This is Yakou-fanatic work!

**HARUTORA**
They're after me?! Why—?

**TOUJI**
Questions later. Move!

**KYOUKO**
That suit man's a Mystical Investigator. The Agency's been watching Natsume-kun.

**TENMA**
A fanatic ambushed Natsume-kun two days before you enrolled. Tried to "awaken" him. The Agency's investigating.

**HARUTORA**
(fist clenching)
He bore that alone... I'm gonna reach him.

**TOUJI**
Counter-curse. I'll trigger the sprinklers to thin the aura—you two cast.

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - TRAINING ARENA - (NATSUME POV)**

Natsume wakes bound, power sealed. The INVESTIGATOR looms.

**INVESTIGATOR**
North Star King. The King has returned to you. I am Hishamaru, your retainer.

**NATSUME**
I am Tsuchimikado Natsume. I am no one's king.

**INVESTIGATOR**
My comrades within the Agency await. And your shikigami... Harutora breathes his last even now.

**NATSUME**
(struggling)
Harutora...! I can't reach his aura—the barrier, the seals—

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CLASSROOM / ARENA - (INTERCUT)**

**TOUJI**
Now—sprinklers!

**KYOUKO**
(casting)
Return, curse, to your caster!

**TENMA**
(gritting)
Got it—!

[Rebounding fog exposes the caster. The group arrives.]

**HARUTORA**
We're taking Natsume back.

**INVESTIGATOR**
Impossible... the vessel turned on me?

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - TRAINING ARENA - CONT.**

**INVESTIGATOR**
(enraged)
You name HIM your only true shikigami?! Then witness—Kakugyouki!

[A giant ONE-ARMED ONI materializes.]

**INVESTIGATOR**
I am Hishamaru. This is Kakugyouki—Yakou's two retainers, reborn!

[The oni's shadow swallows the frame.]

**FADE OUT.**
