**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CLASSROOM - MORNING**

Rows of students; at a window desk sits NATSUME (M), black uniform, black hair in a
pink-ribboned ponytail, alone. KYOUKO KURAHASHI approaches.

**KYOUKO**
I-It's been a while, Natsume-kun. Do you still remember me? I'm Kurahashi Kyouko, from the Kurahashi family.

Natsume looks up. A soft, practiced smile that doesn't fit a boy.

**NATSUME**
Kurahashi. Right. Is there something you need from me?

**KYOUKO**
We met as children, at the Tsuchimikado estate—surely you recall?

**NATSUME**
I don't recall any such thing. If there's nothing else, I'd prefer to be alone.

[Kyouko deflates; the classroom's quiet presses in. Beat.]

**EXT. ONMYO ACADEMY - BUILDING EXTERIOR - MORNING**

Granite walls, scarlet window frames. HARUTORA and TOUJI stand dwarfed by the new school building.

**HARUTORA**
Huh, here? This building? ...I still feel like an outsider.

**TOUJI**
You better prepare your heart.

**HARUTORA**
I was prepared to become an Onmyouji long ago. Probably.

**TOUJI**
Didn't you just correct yourself?

**HARUTORA**
Since I've got the spirit-seeing ability now and I'm Natsume's shikigami, of course I prepared myself! ...Probably.

**TOUJI**
Don't be naive. You're a Tsuchimikado, but a branch one. Everyone reacts the moment they hear the name. Some out of curiosity, some jealousy, some hoping to make a name beating a Tsuchimikado. You might get "eaten."

**HARUTORA**
B-But Natsume's here too—

**TOUJI**
Mendokusai. The main-family heir's a prodigy everyone admires. You showing up late as a branch nobody? You'll draw every eye.

**HARUTORA**
...I'm just an outsider!

**TOUJI**
Doesn't matter. All they care about is the name Tsuchimikado.

[Harutora pales. Camera pushes in on the pentagram mark under his left eye.]

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - ENTRANCE - MORNING**

Between two automatic doors sit stone KOMAINU (Alpha right, Omega left with a horn). They speak.

**ALPHA**
Welcome to the Onmyo Academy. State your names.

**HARUTORA**
I'm Tsuchimikado Harutora.

**TOUJI**
Ato Touji.

**OMEGA**
Voiceprint and aura confirmed and recorded. We have also recorded your shikigami.

**HARUTORA**
My shikigami? I don't have any shikigami!

**TOUJI**
...Maybe it's referring to you being Natsume's shikigami?

**HARUTORA**
That's way too strange a way to put it. Alpha, say it clearly—

**OMEGA**
Our master has notified us: proceed directly to the principal's office.

[The komainu freeze back to stone. Harutora exchanges a look with Touji.]

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE - MORNING**

Taisho-cafe warmth; two-wall bookshelf; a calico CAT on the desk. PRINCIPAL KURAHASHI MIYO, gray, upright in brown kimono, lowers her glasses.

**MIYO**
Welcome. I've been waiting a long time for you two. I am the principal here, Kurahashi Miyo.

**HARUTORA**
H-Hello.

**MIYO**
So you are Natsume-san's Hishamaru and Kakugyouki.

**HARUTORA**
What?!

**MIYO**
I admitted you both as a personal exception, tied to the Dairenji Suzuka incident this summer. I won't deny I also wished to seal your silence on the matter.

**HARUTORA**
A-As I thought...

**MIYO**
You should know the rumors regarding Natsume-san—that he is Tsuchimikado Yakou's reincarnation. Because of them, he is hounded. By a group that worships Yakou as a god. They have even tried to contact him.

**HARUTORA**
Th-They came for Natsume? How—?

**MIYO**
(soft, nostalgic)
He really liked playing shogi. Terribly bad at it, mind you. ...Yes, I knew Yakou. Half a century ago, I was only a child.

**HARUTORA**
You MET Yakou?!

**TOUJI**
You saw Tsuchimikado Yakou when he was alive?

[SFX: cat purrs. Miyo smiles.]

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE / CORRIDOR - MORNING**

OHTOMO JIN limps in on a wooden pirate-style fake leg, Kansai lilt.

**OHTOMO**
Oh? This? It's cool, isn't it? I'm a teacher here, but I'm also an Onmyouji, so I've gotta boast about my prestige.

**MIYO**
This is your homeroom teacher, Ohtomo Jin-sensei. Don't judge him by his appearance—he's extremely capable.

**OHTOMO**
How can you say that, principal. Ah, whatever—hope we get along.

**HARUTORA**
S-Sensei, can I ask something? The principal said "Hishamaru" and "Kakugyouki"—what do those refer to?

**OHTOMO**
Harutora-san, they were the shikigami of Tsuchimikado Yakou. His two retainers.

**HARUTORA**
Huh?!

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CORRIDOR - MORNING**

Ohtomo leads them past glass cases of cursed objects. The cat trails.

**OHTOMO**
Wasn't she scary? That old lady's a big behind-the-scenes figure in this industry.

**HARUTORA**
The principal? You're talking about the principal?

**OHTOMO**
Right. Her shikigami are everywhere, so you'll be caught if you cut class. But if you ever need to—come ask me. I'll tip you off on how to slip past her eyes and ears.

**TOUJI**
(aside to Harutora)
So it was that Kurahashi. I'll explain later.

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CLASSROOM - LATE MORNING**

The room falls silent as Harutora and Touji enter. Every eye turns. Natsume stands.

**KYOUKO**
A branch-family Tsuchimikado, transferring in now? After the main-family heir? That's special treatment if I've ever seen it.

**NATSUME**
(stepping forward, commanding)
He is my shikigami. The Tsuchimikado name grants no privilege he has not earned. Bakatora—stand behind me.

**HARUTORA**
Y-Yes, Natsume!

**KYOUKO**
(narrowing her eyes)
...We'll see whether a late transfer lasts a week.

**HARUTORA**
(under his breath)
I'm really gonna get eaten, aren't I...

**TOUJI**
(dry)
Told you.

[SFX: bell. The class stirs. Camera holds on Harutora's nervous face as the volume rises.]

**FADE OUT.**
