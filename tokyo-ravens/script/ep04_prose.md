# Stage 9 — Light Novel Narration: Episode 4 "Kodoku"

**Style (§5.5):** third-person, toggling between the sealed classroom (Harutora PoV) and the
arena (Natsume POV). Restrained interiority; the menace is in the silence.

---

The afternoon dragged without Natsume. Tenma delivered Ohtomo's shakujou—the one with the
ringing rings—and Harutora's unease hardened into certainty that something was wrong. Then
the window shattered. A kodoku flooded the room: a bug-vessel curse shikigami, forbidden,
alive with venom. A barrier sealed the classroom; no sound would leave.

Kyouko identified it at a glance—Yakou-fanatic work. The attackers were after Harutora.
Between breaths, Kyouko and Tenma filled in the truth: the suit man was a Mystical
Investigator, the Agency had been watching Natsume, and a fanatic had ambushed him two days
before Harutora even enrolled. Natsume had borne it alone. Touji proposed a counter-curse.

In the arena, Natsume woke bound, her power sealed, the Investigator looming over her.
"North Star King," he called her, "I am Hishamaru, your retainer," and spoke of comrades
within the Agency, and of Harutora breathing his last. She couldn't reach Harutora's aura—
the barrier, the seals, the distance.

But the classroom struck back. Touji triggered the sprinklers to thin the aura; Kyouko cast;
Tenma broke the barrier. The curse rebounded as fog, and the caster stood exposed. The group
arrived, and for a heartbeat it seemed won.

Then the Investigator, enraged that Natsume named Harutora her only true shikigami, threw
open a vessel and manifested a giant with one arm. "Kakugyouki," he declared. "I am
Hishamaru. Yakou's two retainers, reborn."

The oni filled the arena, and the episode ended on its shadow.
