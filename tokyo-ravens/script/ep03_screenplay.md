# Stage 10 — Screenplay Adaptation: Episode 3 "Shikigami Showdown"

**Input:** `script/ep03_prose.md` + `script/ep03_dialogue.md`. Format per §5.6. CR-1: female voice ONLY at S3-07.

---

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - TRAINING FIELD (UNDERGROUND) - DAY**

Ringed by seats, wrapped in a First-Class barrier. Harutora in kendo armor + miasma gear, Ohtomo's wooden sword. Kon at his side.

**TOUJI**
So defensive shikigami are status symbols. That's why the class gawked at yours.

**TENMA**
Only the strong can bind one. Natsume-kun and Kyouko-san are the only first-years who have.

**HARUTORA**
Then Kon's... pretty amazing, huh.

**OHTOMO**
(referee)
Arena's a First-Class barrier—contains a third-phase disaster. Fight clean, ya?

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - TRAINING ARENA - DAY**

**HARUTORA**
I'm not just Natsume's shikigami. I'm fighting too. Kon, let's do this!

**KYOUKO**
Foolish. A wooden sword against a Yaksha?

**KON**
Harutora-sama, I will protect you, na no!

[Clash. The wooden sword SHATTERS on Yaksha armor. SFX: crack.]

**KYOUKO**
Done already? I expected more from a Tsuchimikado.

**HARUTORA**
(panting)
Tch... I had a finishing blow lined up, y'know...

**TOUJI**
The sword broke before you could land it. Dumb luck, as always.

**NATSUME**
(under breath, furious)
That idiot...

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - LOCKER ROOM - POST-MATCH**

**NATSUME**
Reckless! You could have been hurt—what were you thinking?!

**HARUTORA**
Is that an order, master?

[Natsume flees, stung.]

**TOUJI**
She said that 'cause she was worried, y'know.

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CLASSROOM - NEXT MORNING**

Harutora shows off Kon, apologizes to a hostile boy. Natsume watches from her seat, excluded.

**TOUJI**
(to Natsume)
Don't force yourself. Remember Hokuto—she died pushing herself too.

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - EMERGENCY STAIRWELL - NOON**

**KYOUKO**
I'm sorry. For the challenge. We met as kids. At the Tsuchimikado estate. You promised me something. You forgot.

**HARUTORA**
Natsume forgot? That's... like him.

**KYOUKO**
The Kurahashi family was once a Tsuchimikado branch family. And you—you chose to be his shikigami, didn't you? Not because of the family.

**HARUTORA**
Yeah. I chose. He's my friend.

**INT. ONMYO ACADEMY - CORRIDOR - NOON (CONT.)**

Natsume finds them.

**NATSUME**
(jealous)
So this is why you cozy up to classmates. Fawning over Kyouko now?

**HARUTORA**
Hah?! I'm not fawning—I was just talking!

**NATSUME**
You're too scared to connect with anyone! You hide behind me!

**HARUTORA**
And you're too scared to let anyone close!

[Natsume's composure cracks; her voice slips into a higher, TRUE register as she turns to flee.]

**NATSUME**
(female voice, breaking)
You—!

[The SUITED INVESTIGATOR steps from shadow: "Stupefy, seal, bind." Natsume drops, sealed, carried off.]

**HARUTORA**
Natsume—?! That voice—!

**FADE OUT.**
